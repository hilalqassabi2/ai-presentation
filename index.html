What is Artificial Intelligence?

Presenter: Haitham

Definition

AI is the ability of a computer to think, learn and make decisions in a way that is similar to humans.

It is a machine that can learn from experience.

Learnfrom data and from experience
Understandlanguage, pictures and sounds
Decidesolve problems and make predictions

How does AI learn?

Presenter: Hilal
DataLots of examples: photos, text, numbers
TrainingThe computer looks for patterns
ModelThe "brain" that remembers the patterns
AnswerIt predicts or decides for new data
Example: Show the computer thousands of photos of cats. It learns the patterns, and then it can find a cat in a photo it has never seen before.

The challenges of AI

Presenter: Khalid
  • JobsSome jobs will change or disappear
  • PrivacyAI collects a lot of personal data

  • Fake contentFake photos, videos and voices
